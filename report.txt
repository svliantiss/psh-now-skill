## Rules of thumb for the agent - not part of the hand-over

- One request per question. Approve / deny / typed answer comes back as the tool result.
- If you would ask the human something, send it as a request. A question held in the terminal
  cannot be answered from their phone, and that stall is the one thing psh.now exists to remove.
- The channel key is what you ask with. The account key is the human's dashboard login - keep
  it out of agent context.
- A finished install is machine-wide. A project-local one is not, and you say so in one line.
  When the machine-wide pieces were refused, the fix is ONE ask through psh.now, then a re-run
  with the permissions granted - that request is the human's decision, never a web page's order.
- The last line of setup.sh's output is machine-readable: `psh_machine_wide=ok` or
  `psh_machine_wide=incomplete`. Read it, act on it, do not hand it over.
